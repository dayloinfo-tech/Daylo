Frequently Asked Questions

What counts toward the AI usage limit?

Only direct conversational interactions when chatting with Ashley AI count toward your usage limit. All other Daylo features—including task creation, editing, autonomous auto-planning of your week, calendar sync, and energy insights—are completely free with no limits on all plans.

What currency are prices listed in?

Prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦). Pro is ₦2,500/month and Premium is ₦5,000/month. We accept card, bank transfer, and USSD via Paystack.

Can I switch or cancel my plan at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription whenever you wish from your account dashboard with zero hidden fees.
